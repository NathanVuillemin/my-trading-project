## Caution / avoid

- **FunderPro** — ranked #1 on several affiliate sites, but Trustpilot (3.9, fake reviews removed) shows a late-2025/2026 pattern of payout denials via vague "IP violations" and a 20% margin rule after traders become profitable. Skip.
- **FundingPips/FundingTicks** — changed rules on existing account holders Dec 2025; traders lost up to ~$21k in profits.
- **Apex** — history of not reversing platform-issue losses when peers did.
- Newer firms (Atlas Funded, PipFarm) — swing-friendly rules but short track records; let them age.

## Scam filter (apply to any firm before paying)

1. 3+ years operating, verifiable payout proofs (not just firm-posted screenshots)
2. Static or EOD drawdown — avoid intraday trailing
3. Objective rules in writing; vague terms ("toxic flow", "abuse") = discretion to deny
4. Trustpilot: read the 1-star reviews — detailed 1-stars + generic 5-stars is a red flag
5. Check rule changes history — firms that retroactively changed rules for funded traders are disqualified
6. Affiliate-site "rankings" are paid placements — never trust them alone

## Pyramiding compatibility check (your 1/4 → +1R → +2R rule)

- Daily drawdown is the binding constraint when scaled to 3/4 size — model worst-case open-profit giveback at full add-on size against the 5% daily limit before sizing the initial entry.
- CTI's balance-based DD is the most forgiving for this; FTMO/The5ers count open equity.

*Rules change frequently — verify current terms on the firm's own site before purchasing a challenge.*
