# 24 · Silver
Condensed summary in my own words. `[Claude+]` = my additions.

## Core points
- **Hybrid metal**: ~58% of demand is industrial (solar/PV ~170Moz is the largest end market; electronics/electrical ~140Moz). Correlations: strongest with gold, weaker with copper/commodities, modest with equities/oil, negative with USD.
- **Supply is inelastic**: ~800–850Moz/yr, +1–2%; **70–75% is a by-product** of copper/zinc/lead mining, so a higher silver price doesn't create supply. Five straight deficit years (latest ~118Moz; ~580Moz cumulative drawdown since 2010). Asian heirloom holdings rarely come back.
- **Mechanically convex**: thin futures, small inventories and tight physical supply mean positioning moves price disproportionately → overshoots both ways. The course describes a recent London squeeze: lease rates >11% at the peak, backwardation, big ETP inflows, a vertical run to an all-time high near ~$120.6, then a ~25–31% one-day crash after margin hikes.
- **Structure**: huge turnover ($5–7tn notional/yr; daily futures volume > a third of annual mine supply; OI 5–7× deliverable stocks; <3% delivered). ~85–90% of spot prices off loco-London. London vaults hold a lot on paper, but the *free float* is much smaller. **ETFs hold 30–40% of investment stock** (vs ~10–12% for gold), so creations and redemptions move lease rates and the curve.
- **Squeeze history**: 1979–80 Hunt brothers corner (~$6 → ~$50 → <$10 after margin hikes and position limits); 2011 QE2 run to ~$50 and back; 2021 retail campaign absorbed by OTC depth; latest scarcity + leverage episode. **Every squeeze ended with a rule change** (margins, limits, financing). Scarcity ≠ liquidity.
- **Wrappers**: spot/ETF (unlevered; allocated vs unallocated), forwards (embed lease rates; e.g. in backwardation sell spot / buy 6m forward to bet on normalisation), futures (incl. new 100oz contract), options (vol at multi-decade highs; skew tradable), **gold/silver ratio** RV, miners (operational leverage).
- **Participants**: producers (hedge sellers into strength → affect velocity), fabricators (buy dips → long-run floor), bullion banks (lease rates = best tightness tell), funds (set the margin price, amplify trends), large retail, **no central-bank floor** (unlike gold).

> `[Claude+]` **For your book**: if you ever add XAGUSD, halve the size vs gold (typically ~1.5–2× gold's volatility). Watch margin-hike announcements from CME: they've marked major silver tops. The gold/silver ratio historically ranged ~40–90+; very high readings have tended to precede silver outperformance in reflation/risk-on phases.
