# 22 · Corporate Bonds
Condensed summary in my own words. `[Claude+]` = my additions.

## Core points
- **Corporate yield = Treasury yield + credit spread** (default + liquidity + fear).
- **IG vs HY** split at BBB−/Baa3. Fallen angels (IG → HY) and rising stars (HY → IG) force index flows; split ratings create pockets of opportunity.
- **Structures**: FRNs (SOFR + spread, little duration), callables (negative convexity, higher coupon), convertibles (see 15), puttables (exit right, lower coupon). Fine print: sinking funds, make-whole calls, refunding protection, **seniority** (senior secured → unsecured → sub → junior). Seniority often drives outcomes more than the entry yield.
- **Reading one bond** (course example: bank bond, 6.4% coupon, issued at +195bp): later trading at ~114 → YTM ~4.9%. Price and yield are the same information.
- **Three spread measures**: nominal (vs one Treasury), **Z-spread** (vs the whole curve), **OAS** (Z minus embedded option value → compare callables and bullets fairly).
- **Default maths**: EL ≈ PD × (1 − R); typical senior unsecured recovery ~40%. Example: 45bp CDS → ~0.15%/yr default probability.
- **Other risks**: rates/duration, liquidity (OTC, no standing bid when dealers step back), call/reinvestment, downgrade, event.
- **Market structure**: OTC, dealer inventory = liquidity; primary syndication vs secondary RFQ/e-platforms.
- **Toolkit**: single-name CDS, CDX IG/HY, bond ETFs (price discovery in stress via NAV discounts), new credit index futures (2023–24).
- **Episodes**: early-2000s IG accounting frauds (trust is part of the spread); 2008 (liquidity breaking point); 2011 (sovereign → corporate contagion); 2014–16 (energy HY: oil $100 → <$30, spreads +1,000bp); Dec 2018 (zero HY issuance for a month); March 2020 (Fed backstop for corporate bonds/ETFs → instant re-tightening).
- **New era: AI capex**: record big-tech IG issuance (single $15–30bn deals; ~$90bn in one autumn; forecasts up to ~$1.5tn by 2028). Even AA hyperscaler paper trades wide of its index; issuers shift into euros (reverse Yankees); speculative issuers pile in; spreads' "base range" drifting higher.

> `[Claude+]` **Macro read for you**: HY OAS (FRED `BAMLH0A0HYM2`) is one of the cleanest risk-regime inputs. Rules of thumb: <350bp calm, 350–500 caution, >500 stress, >800 crisis. Widening HY while equities are flat = early warning (F5). For FX that argues for trimming AUD/NZD longs and favouring JPY/CHF. For gold, stress is supportive unless it turns into a dash for cash.
