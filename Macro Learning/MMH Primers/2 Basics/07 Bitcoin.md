# 07 · Bitcoin
Condensed summary in my own words. `[Claude+]` = my additions.

## Main lessons
- **Clear thinking first**: participants use different signals; your "should" isn't why price moves. A lucky reason for a good trade proves nothing, and narratives are persuasive because they hold a grain of truth. Analyse the seen (price) *and* unseen (supply/demand drivers); re-attribute constantly; respect model ranges; macro knowledge widens the opportunity set vs pure TA.
- **What BTC is**: a security with float, tradable float and volume (exchanges = liquidity funnels). Fixed supply means nothing without known demand. No cash flows and no forced use (unlike taxes forcing USD demand). On-chain data has shown little predictive value.
- **Valuation-only asset**: no earnings engine, so price ≈ the multiple → a near-pure **macro liquidity** expression, similar to gold (with the caveat that perception can change).
- **Macro liquidity = price of money** (rate complex; BTC has high beta to real rates and the real curve) **× quantity of money** (reserves, CB balance sheets) → net impulse.
- **Evidence by era**: 2020–21 negative real rates + surging reserves → melt-up; 2022 contraction → BTC fell despite rising nominal activity (not behaving like a medium of exchange); 2023–24 real rates up but quantity impulse stronger → BTC and gold rallied despite real rates. It's a **liquidity release valve**.
- **Risk curve**: cash → govts → credit → equities → high-beta tech → BTC. Triangulate across the curve and FX (e.g. USDJPY vs Japanese/US equity factors moving with BTC flows).
- **Positioning flows** (ETF flows, liquidation cascades) move within a band before the macro regime reasserts.
- **Strategy skeleton**: liquidity impulse positive → bullish regime → run a momentum/MA strategy (optionally nested buy-the-dip); negative → bearish version. Stops from signal-to-noise/volatility (ATR). Beginners: stick to momentum. **Technical levels exist to be tested**, so don't put stops right on them.

> `[Claude+]` **"Levels exist to be tested"** is the same idea as SMC's liquidity concept: obvious S/R = stop clusters = targets. Your SMC system already places stops beyond sweeps rather than at the level.

> `[Claude+]` **BTC as a gold-sentiment cross-check**: when BTC and gold rally together while real yields rise, it points to a liquidity/debasement bid (as in 2024). When gold rallies and BTC falls, it points to a fear/haven bid. A useful regime tell for your XAUUSD bias.
