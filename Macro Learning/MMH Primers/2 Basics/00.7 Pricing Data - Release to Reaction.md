# 00.7 · From Release to Reaction
Condensed notes in my own words. `[Claude+]` = my additions.

## The gist
Knowing the economy is worthless for trading until it's tied to price reaction. This module is the bridge.

## Key points
- **Triangulate different sources**: price action alone has a ceiling. Combine data, market pricing, surveys and price.
- **Lagged data still matters**: the market knows the lags; every print is read *against* related prints; revisions only matter if they change the expected *future* path.
- **Extrapolation machine**: the market implicitly projects the current speed of the data forward and re-projects after each print (really a distribution, not a single line).
- **3×3 matrix** (print vs consensus × price reaction): the informative cells are the *contradictions*. E.g. hot data but bonds rally (already priced, or shorts flushed); soft data but bonds sell (the market fears something bigger, like inflation). Also check: did the move hold into the close, and how big was it in std devs?
- **Step one is always "what's priced?"**: fed funds futures and inflation swaps give the tradeable forecast. Compare with your view of the likely print → risk/reward skew *before* the release.
- **Routine**: priced → your expected print → release → matrix → hold into close? → size of move.
- **What you're paid for**: carrying uncertainty while it resolves (risk premium). Once something is certain, there's no pay left.

> `[Claude+]` **Matrix for gold (XAUUSD) on US data** (textbook direction):
> | | Gold up | Gold flat | Gold down |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Hot US data (CPI/NFP beat) | **Signal**: fear/haven or de-dollarisation bid dominating | Absorbed | Textbook (real yields ↑, USD ↑) |
> | In line | Something else priced (geopolitics, CB buying) | Textbook | Something else priced |
> | Soft US data | Textbook (yields ↓) | Absorbed | **Signal**: liquidation, or growth fear hitting everything |
> Log each high-impact release in your journal with its matrix cell. After ~30 entries you'll see which contradictions preceded multi-day moves.

> `[Claude+]` **Where to see "priced"**: CME FedWatch (rate path probabilities), 2y Treasury yield (a quick proxy for the expected policy path), 5y5y inflation swap / 10y breakeven, and the implied move from options straddles into CPI/NFP.
