# 00.6 · How Much to Bet
Summary in my own words. `[Claude+]` = my additions.

## Key ideas
- "1–2% per trade" is a lazy default; risk tolerance is personal and usually overestimated. Taking trades ≠ **accepting** their risk. Lack of acceptance → skipped trades, improvising, broken consistency. Confidence is built by surviving drawdowns.
- **Recovery asymmetry**: −10% → +11%, −20% → +25%, −30% → +43%, −40% → +67%, −50% → +100%. Portfolios live mostly in drawdown; drawdown limits are guardrails.
- **Seven considerations**: (1) objectives (whose money, for what, when); (2) starting balance and which accounts form one pool; (3) drawdown limits of four kinds (on principal, on profits, open-profit giveback, losing streaks); (4) construction (max positions, holding periods, asset-class caps, base risk unit, **portfolio heat** = loss if all stops hit); (5) expectancy, trade frequency, return spread, Monte Carlo; (6) **market money**: profit set aside to fund bigger risk; (7) rolling correlations between strategies and versus stocks, bonds, commodities and USD.
- **Example rulebook** (illustrative): base unit 0.40%; step up to 2 units above +10% YTD and 3 units above +15%; max DD 5% on principal and 15% on profits; heat ≤ 10%.
- **Position size = risk budget ÷ (entry − stop)**. Risk ≠ notional: a wide stop gives a small position. In the course's worked examples, the GLD trade had the largest risk budget (1.2%) but the smallest notional (~4%) because its stop was wide.

> `[Claude+]` **Prop-firm translation** (typical 2-step challenge: ~5% daily loss, ~10% max loss):
> - Base unit 0.25–0.5% keeps 10+ consecutive losses survivable.
> - Heat cap ≤ 2% so a correlated gap day can't breach the daily limit.
> - Treat correlated trades as one position (XAUUSD long + EURUSD long + AUDUSD long ≈ one short-USD bet).
> - Scale up only with "market money" after the challenge profit buffer exists, the same logic as the watermarks.

> `[Claude+]` **FX/gold lot sizing**: lots = risk$ ÷ (stop distance × value per unit per lot). XAUUSD: 1 lot = 100 oz, so a $1 move = $100/lot. $500 risk with a $10 stop → 0.5 lots. EURUSD: 1 lot ≈ $10/pip, so $500 risk with a 25-pip stop → 2 lots.
