# 02 · Equities — the S&P 500 as a system
Condensed summary in my own words. `[Claude+]` = my additions.

## Main lessons
- Prices are the footprints of decisions by people on every horizon. The survival skill is **adaptation**, driven by clearly quantified signals (signal-to-noise falls as complexity rises).
- **Three pillars**: top-down GIP regime + bottom-up fundamentals → *why/direction*; quant signals → *when*; risk management absorbs errors in both.
- **GIP quadrants** (rough multi-decade tendencies):
  | | Inflation falling | Inflation rising |
  |---|---|---|
  | Growth rising | Stocks and HY credit lead ("sweet spot") | Commodities/oil, EM lead; bonds hurt |
  | Growth falling | Long govt bonds, quality credit | **Stagflation**: gold and energy hold; stocks *and* bonds struggle |
- Decompose index moves into growth, inflation, liquidity and discount rate (earnings ↔ G/I; multiple ↔ liquidity). Track YoY/3m/MoM and surprises.
- **Positioning is in the price**: trust instruments with money at stake; watch reactions that contradict the data (e.g. soft inflation data but bonds can't hold a rally).
- **Bottom-up kit**: earnings surprises vs economic surprises by sector; index vs 10y yield (the stock–bond correlation flips by regime); sector ratios (e.g. materials/tech vs inflation); factor behaviour by regime; short interest (squeeze vs real buying); earnings-call commentary.
- **The index isn't the market**: cap-weighting lets a few mega-caps outvote the rest. Watch breadth (% above MAs, new highs/lows), cap- vs equal-weight ratio (index lagging equal-weight *and* falling breadth has historically preceded more downside), tech/index ratio, and the full distribution of member returns.
- **Vol complex**: the 00.4 dials plus VIX term structure (spot > futures = stress now), implied correlation, dispersion, and option OI vs D/W/M closes. Options make the need for timing explicit.
- **Trend vs mean reversion**: the S&P leans mean-reverting (hence dip-buying works often). No one "owns" the definition of a trend; quantify your own. Starter idea: VIX regime model + smoothed slope for inflections + momentum.
- Intraday: study overnight, first 30 min, midday, last hour separately per regime.
- **Pipeline**: inputs → signals → backtest → regimes → backtest regimes → quant timing. When price disagrees with your thesis, **revise the thesis**.

> `[Claude+]` **Why equities matter for your FX/gold book**: S&P/Nasdaq direction is the fastest risk-sentiment proxy. Risk-on equities usually mean AUD/NZD bid and JPY/CHF offered. A falling SPX with VIX term structure inverted (VIX > VIX3M) is a classic risk-off tell. Consider it an input to your 00.9 regime filter.

## Self-test
1. What are the three pillars and the job of each?
2. Which quadrant is worst for a 60/40 portfolio, and what holds up there?
3. The index is up 0.5% while 380 of 500 stocks are down. How?
4. What does spot VIX above VIX futures signal?
5. Why does dip-buying often work on the S&P?
6. Price keeps going against your fundamental thesis. What does the course say to do?
