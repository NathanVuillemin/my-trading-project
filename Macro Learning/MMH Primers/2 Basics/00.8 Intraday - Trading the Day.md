# 00.8 · Trading the Day
Condensed notes in my own words. `[Claude+]` = my additions.

## The gist
Intraday is the **hardest** timeframe. Frame it as *liquidity provision*, not pattern-hunting.

## Key points
- **Rules ≠ the machine**: a setup can work until the underlying cause changes. The edge is knowing *why* it works and spotting when regimes change (the Soros skill). Be a strategy *factory*, a research lab, not a one-formula trader. Poker rule: if you can't spot the sucker, it's you.
- **Why price moves**: not enough resting liquidity on the other side, so aggressive size has to walk the book. **Metaorder footprint**: price pushed during execution, partial snap-back after completion. Liquidity is uneven through time.
- **Know the table**: HFT (µs), stat-arb (s–min), market makers, institutions. The 5–15 min chart is the danger zone. Imbalanced order flow is the *normal* state.
- **Two real edges**: (1) information not in price (e.g. forecasting data surprises, forced unwinds); short-lived and better on ~1-week horizons. (2) **Different time preference**: hold hours or into the close and profit from forced executors (day-end/week-end flatteners). Think of your fill as a service you sell.
- **Building blocks**: session split (overnight, first 30 min, first half, midday lull, second half, last 30 min); volume; TWAP vs VWAP deviation (don't over-weight VWAP); OHLC reference levels (day/week/month) with std-dev distances. Ask conditional questions, e.g. odds of reversal at X std devs from the weekly open.
- **Volume magnets**: the open and close. Thin-volume deviations tend to mean-revert as closing volume arrives; if they *don't*, the move is durable.
- **Author's stance**: macro edges rarely show up intraday → use this mainly to improve *execution* of higher-timeframe trades.
- **Options flow**: crowded. Gamma matters when hedge-required OI > average daily volume. OI strikes are *not* S/R walls. Net positions out and read against vol regime and expiry calendar. 0DTE is overhyped.

> `[Claude+]` **Mapping to FX/gold (no cash open/close)**: the equivalent volume magnets are the **London open (08:00 UK)**, the **NY open / US data at 8:30 ET**, the **London 4pm fix (WM/Reuters)**, and the **LBMA gold price auctions (10:30 & 15:00 London)**. Month-end London fix = big rebalancing flows. Your kill zones in SMC line up with these liquidity windows, which is the mechanical *why* behind them.

> `[Claude+]` **Practical for your system**: treat the M5/M15 as *execution* only; the edge should come from the H4/D bias + macro layer. That matches the course's advice to trade above the machines.
