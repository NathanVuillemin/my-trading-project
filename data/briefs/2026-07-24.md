  and XYZ100 (-$11.45M). Everything positive in the index rollup is covering.
- **Equities** — the only class where headline and fresh risk agree in sign, but the magnitude is small
  (+$3.50M fresh of +$11.98M net). Two-thirds of equity "buying" was closing shorts. Semis are internally
  split: SKHX/SKHY/CXMT/SNDK bought, MU/INTC sold.
- **Commodities** — +$3.42M fresh long, but essentially one crude position.
- **FX** — $15.9k on one market. Statistically empty; ignore.

Reminder that stocks, indices, commodities and FX trade on a **separate Hyperliquid perp dex** and are
invisible to a default `clearinghouseState` query — position-level follow-up on any of the above needs
`"dex":"xyz"`.

## 8. Caveats

- **Cold start — floor-only, no baseline yet.** `baselineDays: 0` and `z: null` on all three coin flags.
  These cleared a dollar floor and nothing else. There is **no** statistical significance here; treat
  "outlier" as "large", not "unusual". `classFlags` is empty.
- **Single snapshot in the entire history.** 2026-07-24 is the only file in both directories. No
  day-over-day comparison is possible and nothing here is a *change* from a prior state.
- **The `run.log` disagrees with the JSON.** The log is from an earlier run (21:28 UTC) than the
  persisted snapshot (22:23 UTC): it reports CRYPTO -$0.23M vs -$0.75M, STOCK +$14.47M vs +$11.98M, and
  flags AMD as a fourth outlier which the JSON does not. All figures in this brief come from the JSON.
- **Attribution overwhelms the flags.** With 42% of gross flow in one address, most "consensus" labels in
  the raw output do not survive section 4's size filter. Stated plainly: this is one desk's day.
- **Only top 6 drivers per coin are persisted**, so wallet aggregates in section 5 are lower bounds on
  gross activity and may miss smaller participants.
- **25 of 150 wallets returned no fills** (125 with fills). Absence is not flat — it may be API failure.
- **Position age not verified.** Instruction was to use on-disk data only, so no live calls were made.
  Whether `0xec4a…cf62`'s +$23M is a new position or the unwind of an old one is **unresolved** and is
  the single highest-value follow-up. To settle it:
  `POST https://api.hyperliquid.xyz/info` with
  `{"type":"userFillsByTime","user":"0xec4a6f59960fb55a7fa49262e2628687b322cf62","startTime":<ms>}`
  and `{"type":"clearinghouseState","user":"0xec4a…","dex":"xyz"}` for the index leg. If the fills window
  caps out, the position may be older than it appears.
- **Lighter's premium score is negative on all 9 assets** (-0.047% to -0.109%). Nine uniformly negative
  readings is more consistent with a venue-wide basis/mark artefact than nine independent bearish
  signals, and it drags every composite down by a roughly constant amount. Similarly `okx-top` reads
  above 1.0 on 7 of 9 — top-trader L/S ratios sit structurally long. Neither is being treated here as an
  independent directional read.
- **Crowd sources are not confirmation.** 7 of the 9 composite assets have no Tier A per-wallet data at
  all (only ETH, BTC, SOL do); LINK, AVAX, XRP, HYPE, BNB, DOGE rest on `lighter`/`aster`/`okx-top`,
  i.e. market structure plus one aggregate ratio. Those composite scores are weak by construction.
- **No cross-venue coverage for the day's actual action.** Smart-money tracks 9 crypto assets only. The
  index and equity flow that dominates this snapshot has **no** second-source confirmation available.
